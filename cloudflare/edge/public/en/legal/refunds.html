Refund Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. All purchases are final

Service Credits purchased through Veyl are prepaid credit for the Service. Except as described in sections 2 and 3, purchases are final and non-refundable. Service Credits are not a deposit, have no cash value, and cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or redeemed for cash.

Unused Service Credits are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase; they expire afterward and are not refunded.

2. When we do refund

We will refund a payment, or add the Service Credits, if:

  • you were charged twice for the same purchase; or
  • a refund is required by applicable law.

Contact us at support@dessage.xyz with the transaction email and approximate time. We aim to respond within five business days.

If you have not consumed any Service Credits and have not deployed any server, contact us; we will review your case under the mandatory consumer-protection rules that apply to you.

3. Orders from the EEA and the UK

We do not currently offer the Service to consumers in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom. We may refuse or cancel orders from the EEA or the UK; if a payment was made, we will refund it in full to the original payment method.

4. Violation and forfeiture

If we reasonably determine that you have violated the Terms of Service, we may, to the extent permitted by law, suspend or terminate your account, destroy servers created through the Service, and forfeit unused Service Credits without refund.

5. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please contact us before contacting your bank or payment provider. A chargeback opened without contacting us may result in suspension of the account while the dispute is reviewed.

6. Contact

Refund requests: support@dessage.xyz.
