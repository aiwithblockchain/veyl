This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service.

2. What we collect

CategoryExamplesWhy
Account data Email address, verification method, device identifier, session token To create and secure your account and keep you signed in

3. Server credentials

When we create a server for you, we generate access credentials and store what is needed to operate and reconnect that server. These credentials are used only to provide the Service.

We do not inspect or log the content of traffic that passes through your server.

If you export a configuration to a third-party client on a phone, that client and your use of it are not part of the Service. They are handled by the client's provider under its own privacy policy.

4. AI assistant

The AI assistant uses an API key that you provide. The key is stored only in the operating system keychain on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. Requests made by the assistant go directly from your device to the AI provider you configured, and are governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy.

5. Cookies and local storage

Our website does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking, and we do not run any analytics. The site stores only your language preference in your browser's local storage (key veyl.lang). The desktop client keeps the login session token and your own configuration on your device.

6. How we use data

  • to provide, secure, and improve the Service;
  • to calculate charges and maintain your Service Credits;
  • to detect abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access;
  • to comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations;
  • to respond to support requests.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

7. Sharing

We share data only with providers that help us run the Service, including:

  • payment processors (including Stripe), to take payments, screen for fraud, and handle disputes;
  • cloud infrastructure providers, to create and operate servers;
  • email and hosting providers, to deliver login codes and operate the website;
  • authorities, where required by law.

8. Retention

Account, top-up, and charge records are kept for 30 days. Payment and billing details are retained by Stripe; you can find them in your Stripe receipts. Technical logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted or aggregated. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to resolve the request.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email support@dessage.xyz. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to request access to, deletion of, or correction of your personal data, and to exercise these rights without discrimination. We do not sell personal data.

10. International transfers

We and our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. We apply appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

11. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
