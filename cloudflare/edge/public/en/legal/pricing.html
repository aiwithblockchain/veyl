Pricing

Last updated: November 23, 2026

How billing works

Veyl uses prepaid Service Credits. You buy Service Credits in one of the fixed amounts below, and each running server deducts its daily rate from them. There is no subscription and no automatic renewal. Destroying a server ends its charges.

Charges are calculated per running server per calendar day. They do not depend on traffic, bandwidth, or usage.

Deductions

  • The daily rate is deducted once per running server per day.
  • Several servers can draw from the same Service Credits.
  • If your Service Credits cannot cover the next day, the server is suspended and then destroyed.
  • Suspended or destroyed servers are not charged.

Expiry

  • Service Credits are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. Unused Credits expire and are not refunded.
  • Service Credits can only be used to pay for the Service. They cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or redeemed for cash.

Availability

We do not currently offer the Service to consumers in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom. We may refuse or cancel orders from the EEA or the UK; if a payment was made, we will refund it in full to the original payment method.

Payment methods

Card payments are supported, together with local wallets where available at checkout (currently Alipay and WeChat Pay). All payments are one-time; no card is stored for recurring charges.

Taxes

Prices are shown in US dollars and exclude taxes unless stated otherwise at checkout. Where tax applies, it is calculated and collected by our payment provider.

Changes

We may change prices in the future. Changes apply to purchases made after the new price takes effect and do not reduce Service Credits you already hold.
